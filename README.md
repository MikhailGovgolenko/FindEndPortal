<h1 align="center">FindEndPortal</h1>

<p align="center"> https://mikhailgovgolenko.github.io/FindEndPortal/</p>

A portable utility to calculate trajectories and locate Minecraft Strongholds using Eyes of Ender.

<img width="1536" height="863" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/41fbc128-a1d0-40d0-8723-106fb3745246" />




### How to use:

1. **Run** FindEndPortal in your browser, or launch it as a native desktop/PWA application.
2. Throw the first **Eye of Ender**, then input your current X/Z coordinates and exact angle into the first card.
3. Move away from your initial position (ideally 100–200 blocks perpendicular to the first throw).
4. Throw the second **Eye of Ender** and input the new coordinates and angle into the second card.
5. View the precise coordinates of the Stronghold, which are **calculated and updated automatically** in the result panel as you type.

---

This application is fully standalone, requires no installation, works completely offline, and leaves no traces on your system.
